# 51. Phase 5 Prototype Acceptance

The CV09 evidence prototype and subsequent holistic CV09 section mock-up have been reviewed and accepted as the governing report-development grammar.

The accepted reader sequence is:

> **Explain → Prove → Interpret → Act / Transition**

The prototype demonstrates that the report can preserve formal engineering evidence discipline without allowing photographic evidence to overpower the reader-facing message.

The accepted higher-level report identity remains:

1. **Formal shell** — governance, issue control, scope, limitations, references and appendices.
2. **Narrative core** — the audit story and engineering logic communicated in a way that is usable by readers from the shop floor through engineering management.
3. **Technical evidence backbone** — controlled photographs, video-derived frames, evidence IDs, qualifications and action details used to substantiate the narrative.

The evidence component is therefore not the report story by itself. It is the controlled proof unit embedded within the wider narrative.

# 52. Accepted Evidence-Module Grammar

## 52.1 Story before evidence

The report should explain the local/system engineering proposition before the reader is asked to interpret detailed photographs.

The story layer should answer:
- what is happening;
- why it matters;
- how the local case relates to the wider audit.

## 52.2 Evidence as proof

The evidence module then establishes the technical proposition using the minimum E/S evidence required for the reader to verify the point.

The module should not repeat the entire preceding story. Its purpose is proof and traceability.

## 52.3 Interpretation after evidence

After the proof unit, the narrative should state:
- what the evidence means;
- what remains unproven or unmeasured;
- why the case matters beyond the local asset.

## 52.4 Action / transition

The section should then either:
- convert the finding into a local action; or
- use the local case as the bridge into the wider engineering control philosophy.

This prevents the report from falling back into a photograph-by-photograph defect-register rhythm.

# 53. Reader-Level Acceptance

The accepted architecture must continue to work for multiple reader levels.

### Shop-floor / maintenance reader
Should be able to recognise:
- the physical condition;
- the affected equipment/location;
- what immediate maintenance/inspection action is expected.

### Engineering reader
Should be able to follow:
- mechanism;
- evidence;
- qualification;
- measurement/design-development requirement;
- recommended engineering control.

### Management reader
Should be able to understand:
- the system-level problem;
- why recurring housekeeping alone is insufficient;
- the major local exceptions;
- the investment/control philosophy;
- the priority sequence.

No reader should be forced to process the entire detailed evidence register to understand the report conclusion.

# 55. Phase 6 Working Priorities

The Phase 6 objective is **structural and technical completeness**, not final visual styling.

During assembly:
- preserve the formal report shell;
- draft the narrative continuously from beginning to conclusion;
- use evidence modules according to narrative need;
- apply full modules only to complex cases;
- use compact modules or montage/comparator treatment for supporting cases;
- preserve canonical evidence IDs;
- preserve video provenance;
- keep direct observation, visual support and engineering inference distinct;
- ensure recommendations trace back to observed mechanisms and evidence.

Final document-wide styling — fonts, colours, final evidence bars, caption styling, TOC/LOF/LOT presentation and final page cosmetics — should be deferred until the content architecture is stable to avoid double handling.

# 56. Evidence-Density Principle After Prototype Review

The accepted CV09 layout confirms that evidence density should be driven by the engineering story rather than by a fixed photographs-per-page rule.

Use three practical module levels:

1. **Full complex module** — CV09, Screens, L1, Sample Conveyor 3, U4, W10.
2. **Compact technical module** — CV06, Sample Conveyor 2, L3 and selected water cases.
3. **Supporting/comparator evidence** — Crusher, UC route, U3, U5A/U5B, CV02/CV03/CV14, L2/L4 and other assets whose primary value is recurrence, extent, topology or comparison.

E classification indicates essential proof value, not an obligation that every E image must consume body space. S evidence remains available where it materially improves the story. A evidence remains controlled for traceability, appendix/reference use and design-development support.

# 57. Governing Engineering Message for Assembly

The report must continue to converge on the higher-order conclusion:

> **Keep the combined product — fines and coarse/lump ore — within the intended process/material-handling envelope throughout transit, using the control mechanism appropriate to the failure mode and local geometry.**

Supporting principles remain:

> **Do not merely clean the belt. Keep the fines inside the process.**

and

> **Housekeeping controls the consequence. Source control should reduce the recurrence.**

The report must also retain the central evidentiary principle that the location at which spilled material is found is not necessarily the location at which the initiating failure occurred.
