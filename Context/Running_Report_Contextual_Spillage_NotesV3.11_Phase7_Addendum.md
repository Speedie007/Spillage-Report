> **Housekeeping controls the consequence. Source control should reduce the recurrence.**

The report must continue to distinguish:

- system-wide recurring mechanisms from local exceptions;
- active loss-of-containment mechanisms from deposits left by earlier events;

# 65. Priority Success / Acceptance Criteria

## 65.1 CV09 cleaner and containment pilot

Success is functional rather than cosmetic. The pilot should demonstrate, subject to agreed monitoring conditions:

- reduced downstream carryback relative to the pre-trial condition;
- removed wet fines routed into the intended process stream rather than the plant area;
- stable cleaner-to-belt contact through the agreed wear range;
- no unacceptable belt damage or abnormal wear;
- maintainable inspection and adjustment access;
- no new trapping, guarding or safety conflict;
- a monitoring period and inspection record sufficient to judge performance.

The pilot standardises the **required function**, not necessarily identical hardware at every conveyor.

## 65.2 U4 tracking / alignment

Required outcome:

- relevant pulley/alignment geometry measured and corrected where required;
- belt tracking verified during representative operation and across full tripper travel;
- adequate side clearance / carrying margin demonstrated;
- one-sided product-loss condition materially reduced or removed;
- local cleaner arrangement assessed separately from alignment correction.

## 65.3 Sample Conveyors 2 and 3

Required outcome:

- usable belt width documented;
- representative feed quantity and product-size condition verified;
- Sample 3 lateral offset measured and mechanical contributors checked;
- tracking/feed issues corrected before secondary containment is finalised;
- any side containment maintains belt clearance, access and maintainability.

Sample Conveyor 2 mistracking remains unconfirmed and must not be promoted to fact.

## 65.4 W10

Required outcome:

- normal belt tracking envelope established;
- trough, belt, support and clearance geometry verified;
- initiating contributors investigated;
- any side-containment concept shown not to become a normal belt-contact surface;
- access, trapping, wear and maintenance risks assessed.

The side barrier / rail remains a feasibility concept, not a fabrication design.

## 65.5 Screens and L1 structural work

Required outcome:

- competent structural assessment completed;
- present condition, remaining capacity and required repair/replacement scope defined;
- Screens water escape mechanism identified before detailed modification;
- structural repair recommendations kept separate from unsupported assumptions about original cause.
