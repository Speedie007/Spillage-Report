# 2. Relationship to the Locked V4.2 Technical Concept

The two figures are intentionally simple.

They do **not** need to depict every locked functional requirement.

The written V4.2 Section 14 remains the controlling technical specification for:

- pulley-supported cleaner contact;
- two-stage preferred/default arrangement;
- one-stage constrained minimum where verified constraints prevent two;
- full-width transverse cleaner support/cross-arm;
- support/guided movement through both chute side walls;
- external self-tensioning;
- positive travel/wear limitation;
- stable/reasonably uniform cleaner contact across the effective belt width;
- safe maintainability/access;
- natural retention of removed fines within the existing discharge flow;
- OEM/detail-design responsibility for component design and fabrication.

Therefore:

> **DRAW-005 answers “where / orientation?”**
>
> **DRAW-006 answers “inside which containment / why?”**
>
> **V4.2 Section 14 answers “how must the cleaner system function?”**

This three-part relationship is the accepted CV09 communication method for V0.6 development.
