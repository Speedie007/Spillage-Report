# 1. Locked CV09 Functional Concept

The accepted concept is:

> **The preferred CV09 cleaner concept is a two-stage transverse cleaner arrangement installed within the head/discharge chute, with both cleaner stages acting against the conveyor belt while it remains positively supported by the head pulley. Each full-width scraper blade is carried on a transverse support extending through guided openings in both chute side walls. External self-tensioning arrangements maintain controlled cleaner contact as the wear elements reduce, while positive mechanical travel stops limit the available wear-compensation movement. Material removed by either cleaner stage falls naturally within the existing head chute and remains with the combined-product discharge flow, preventing residual fines from being carried onto the return side of the belt. Where verified installation constraints prevent two cleaning stages, at least one effective pulley-supported stage is required. Final mechanical design and component specification remain the responsibility of the OEM/detailed designer.**

# 3. Additional Technical Clarifications Locked During Closure

## 3.1 Pulley-supported location

Both cleaner stages should act on the belt while it is positively supported by the head pulley. This provides a stable reaction surface and reduces the ability of the belt to deflect away from the cleaning force.

## 3.2 Full transverse support

The cleaner is not a one-sided/cantilevered arm. Each full-width blade is mounted on a transverse support/cross-arm spanning the chute/belt and protruding through both side walls.

## 3.3 Guided chute-wall slots

The cross-arm ends pass through guided slots/openings in both chute side walls. These openings form part of the wear-compensation travel path for the complete scraper assembly.

## 3.4 External tensioning

Spring/tensioning hardware is outside the chute. Functional loading should maintain stable/reasonably uniform contact across the effective belt width; the OEM determines how the two sides are linked, adjusted or balanced.

## 3.5 Natural discharge containment

The concept does not create a special scraper-fines chute. Removed material is already inside the head chute and falls by gravity into the same discharge flow as the combined product.

## 3.6 Wet and dry residual fines

The concept applies to residual fines irrespective of whether they are wet/sticky or dry. CV09 wet fines provide strong evidence of the current failure, but the cleaner function is broader.

## 3.7 CV06 / CV09 / CV15 functional lessons

- **CV06:** demonstrates cleaner mechanical-integrity / maintenance failure.
- **CV09:** demonstrates location / containment / cleaning-performance failure and is the functional pilot.
- **CV15:** demonstrates downstream cleaner-effectiveness concern; exact local cleaner failure mechanism still requires verification.

These locations support standardising the required cleaner function rather than one identical hardware package.
