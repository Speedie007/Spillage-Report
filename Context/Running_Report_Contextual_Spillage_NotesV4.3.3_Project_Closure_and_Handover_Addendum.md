## 3. Final engineering/message controls retained in the issued report

The issued report closes on the following accepted engineering interpretation:

- fines accumulation was observed throughout the inspected conveyor ecosystem, with severity and local mechanism varying by location;
- carryback is the strongest recurring fines mechanism but is not claimed as the sole proven cause of every deposit;
- deposit location is not automatically the initiating cause location;
- housekeeping addresses accumulated consequence while source control aims to reduce recurrence;
- CV09 is the pilot/reference location for demonstrating a cleaner-and-containment **functional standard**, with wider application based on local applicability rather than identical hardware;
- W10 side skirting/spill sealing is the preferred engineered lateral-containment addition for the demonstrated W10 condition, with final detail based on verified loading, tracking and geometry;
- Sample Conveyors 2 and 3 use the same side-containment principle after feed/product/loading/tracking/alignment contributors are quantified and corrected or adjusted where required;
- U4 and L3 remain location-specific alignment/tracking cases;
- Screens water escape and structural deterioration remain separate verification/competent-assessment workstreams;
- L1 structural and belt-clearance issues remain subject to competent assessment and representative verification;
- representative operating performance, not work completion or a temporarily clean condition, is the governing close-out principle.

Outstanding measurements, detailed design, OEM review, structural assessment and operating verification identified in the report are **client implementation/close-out requirements**. They are not unfinished report-development work and do not reopen the Revision 1.5 report.
