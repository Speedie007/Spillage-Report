## Engineering / Narrative Outcome

The S-pass was deliberately used to reduce narrative duplication after the principal engineering context had already been established during the earlier E review.

The review retained S status where an item materially adds one or more of the following:
- a distinct mechanism or process relationship;
- operating or dynamic context that the essential stills do not provide;
- a useful severity/extent bridge between principal images;
- a clean-condition or operating comparator that prevents overstatement;
- geometry or design detail required to explain the engineering interpretation or later remedial concept.

Items were moved from S to A where they remained technically valid but primarily served as:
- alternate severity/extent views already carried by stronger E or retained S evidence;
- secondary topology/orientation records;
- duplicate clean-condition comparators;
- alternate defect views useful for traceability but not needed for the reader-facing story;
- contextual evidence that may be useful during detailed design, verification or appendix assembly but does not need body-story priority.

No item was moved to X. This is intentional rather than an incomplete exclusion exercise. The curated evidence set had already been reduced substantially before matrix compilation, and the reviewed demoted items remain legitimate technical records. The A layer is therefore used to retain them without forcing them into the report narrative.

## Final Evidence-Pool Meaning

The final classification should be interpreted as follows:

- **E — Essential body evidence:** principal proof required to establish the audit story or a material engineering finding.
- **S — Supporting / story evidence:** materially strengthens, explains or connects the essential evidence and remains available for body use where the evidence module benefits from it.
- **A — Appendix / reference evidence:** technically valid controlled evidence retained for traceability, alternate views, orientation, comparator support or optional layout use.
- **X — Exclude:** no current items. This category remains available only if a later quality-control review identifies an item that is genuinely unusable or non-contributory.

`E + S = 119` media items form the current **story-capable evidence pool**. This does **not** mean all 119 items must appear in the main report. Final selection, grouping and image size remain Phase 5/6 layout decisions.

The `A = 78` media items remain part of the controlled technical evidence record and may still be used selectively in appendices, evidence registers, design-development discussions or where final layout requires an alternate view.

## Governing Evidentiary Discipline Preserved

The matrix closure does not change the established engineering qualifications. The final report must continue to distinguish:
- visually proven conditions;
- inspector-recorded direct observations;
- source-recorded operating or maintenance information;
- engineering interpretation / hypothesis;
- working remedial concepts;
- final recommendation candidates;
- unresolved items requiring verification or measurement.

Observed deposit location must not automatically be treated as root-cause location, and visible severity must not be converted into an unsupported quantified loss rate.
