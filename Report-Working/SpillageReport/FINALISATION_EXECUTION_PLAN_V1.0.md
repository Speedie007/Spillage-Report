# Phase 2 — Two-perspective report impact review

**Checklist source:** interpret the report once as a non-technical reader and once as a technical reader.

Perform this only after Phase 1 changes are incorporated.

## 2A. Non-technical reader pass

Test whether a reader without conveyor-engineering background can accurately explain:

- [ ] What the mine-wide problem is.
- [ ] Why fines are part of the product but loss of fines from the process is the problem.
- [ ] Why a deposit location may not be the initiating cause location.
- [ ] Why housekeeping alone is not the long-term solution.
- [ ] Why fines presence is system-wide but the exact mechanism/severity varies by location.
- [ ] What CV09 is intended to prove and what happens after a successful pilot.
- [ ] Why W10/skirting is a different, location-specific control philosophy.
- [ ] Why Sample Conveyors require feed/product/loading/tracking verification before residual containment is finalised.
- [ ] What U4, L3, Screens and L1 require next.
- [ ] What the mine should do first and how success will be judged.

Record any paragraph that depends on hidden author knowledge or uses terminology a reasonable non-technical reader cannot follow.

## 2B. Technical/engineering reader pass

Test whether a technical reviewer can confirm:

- [ ] Every material causal statement is supported at the correct evidence level.
- [ ] Direct observation, visual support, source-recorded information, engineering interpretation and unresolved verification remain distinct.
- [ ] The report does not claim carryback is the sole cause of every fines deposit.
- [ ] The CV09 system standard is functional, not an identical-hardware mandate.
- [ ] The skirting hierarchy correctly separates primary contributors from residual containment.
- [ ] Structural findings do not imply remaining capacity without competent assessment.
- [ ] Screens water escape is directly observed but the internal failure mechanism remains unresolved pending shutdown inspection.
- [ ] U4 and L3 evidence boundaries remain distinct.
- [ ] Actions include measurable/observable representative-operation acceptance criteria where appropriate.
- [ ] Open OEM/responsible-engineer/South32 responsibilities are clearly allocated and not presented as audit-completed facts.

## 2C. Backward traceability test

For each major Executive Summary and Conclusion message:

- [ ] identify the body section that develops the finding;
- [ ] identify the action/programme response; and
- [ ] confirm the appendices/evidence support it where applicable.

**Exit criterion:** both readers derive the intended message, and no conclusion outruns the body evidence or action programme.
