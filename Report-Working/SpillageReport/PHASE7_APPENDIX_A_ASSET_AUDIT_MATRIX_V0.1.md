## Purpose
Appendix A provides a concise asset-by-asset audit matrix for all **23 controlled evidence groups**. It is intended to preserve full asset traceability without forcing the main report body back into a flat defect register.

The matrix summarises the inspected condition, current engineering interpretation, evidence qualification and action route. Detailed media-by-media evidence classification remains controlled in `EVIDENCE_STORY_MATRIX_V0.1.csv` and the six `evidence_visual_review_batch_*.md` files.

## Control Notes
1. The matrix is a **summary register**, not a substitute for the per-item controlled evidence record.
2. Direct visual condition, source-recorded observation, engineering interpretation and unresolved verification items remain distinct.
3. Asset classifications must not be escalated from a single severe close view where the wider evidence set supports a lower route-level classification.
4. No dimension, tolerance, capacity, feed rate or OEM criterion is introduced here unless separately verified.
5. Major action details and functional acceptance criteria remain controlled in `PHASE7_PART_XI_RECOMMENDED_ACTION_PROGRAMME_DRAFT_V0.3.md` and `PHASE7_VERIFICATION_SUCCESS_CRITERIA_REGISTER_V0.1.md`.
