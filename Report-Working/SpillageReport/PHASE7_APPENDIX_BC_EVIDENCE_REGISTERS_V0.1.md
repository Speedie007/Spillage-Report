## B3. Photographic evidence handling
- Repository originals remain the master evidence.
- Final report body should use the **smallest evidence set that clearly proves the required point**.
- Unused but controlled photographs remain available through the evidence matrix/appendix traceability.
- A still photograph can prove a physical condition without proving the complete dynamic mechanism or causal history.
- Report-ready resized/compressed copies may be created only for figures actually inserted into the final report; originals remain unchanged.
