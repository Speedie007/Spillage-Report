## CV09 figure-pair rule

The two active CV09 figures have deliberately separate roles:

- **DRAW-005 = placement / orientation.** It shows where typical primary and secondary cleaner stages act in relation to the head pulley and head chute.
- **DRAW-006 = containment relationship.** It shows that both cleaner stages should be inside the head/discharge chute so removed residual fines fall into the existing combined-product discharge flow.

Neither drawing is intended to depict every V4.2 mechanical requirement. The written V4.2 functional specification remains controlling for:

- two-stage preferred/default arrangement;
- one-stage minimum only where verified constraints prevent two;
- pulley-supported cleaner contact;
- full transverse cleaner support across the effective belt width;
- guided support movement through both chute side walls;
- external self-tensioning;
- positive travel/wear limitation;
- stable/reasonably uniform contact;
- maintainability/access;
- OEM responsibility for detailed component/fabrication design.
