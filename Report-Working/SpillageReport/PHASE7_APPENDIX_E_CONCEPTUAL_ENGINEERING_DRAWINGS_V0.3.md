# W10 Figure-Pair Rule

The accepted W10 figure set deliberately separates site context from the skirting mechanism:

- **DRAW-0010 — W10 Side-Skirting / Product-Containment Concept:** retains the tunnel/person context and shows the side-skirt / spill-seal arrangement in relation to the W10 troughed conveyor.
- **DRAW-0011 — Conveyor Side Skirting / Spill Seal Concept — Cross-Section:** enlarged explanatory view showing the skirt/seal elements descending on the inside of the carrying width along both sides of the troughed belt.

The governing functional objective is:

> **Keep the transported product on the belt by using side skirting / spill sealing within the conveyor carrying width, while separately investigating and correcting loading, tracking or other primary contributors where present.**

The old external-barrier interpretation is no longer controlling.

The W10 tunnel/access route remains relevant as an **as-found context and consequence of spillage**, but the proposed skirt is not defined as a walkway-protection barrier.

## W10 report-use language

Although the controlled repository filenames retain `Side_Containment_Barrier_Principle`, report captions and narrative should use terms such as:

- **side skirting**;
- **spill seal**;
- **side-containment skirting**;
- **product-containment skirting**.

Avoid describing DRAW-0010 or DRAW-0011 as a freestanding external barrier.

---

# W10 Technical Boundary

The report may define the intended function and arrangement:

- skirting/sealing on both conveyor sides where required;
- flexible/soft belt-friendly containment element;
- positioned within the conveyor carrying width along the belt edge;
- close or controlled contact with the belt as required by the selected OEM system;
- compatible with normal troughed-belt tracking/movement;
- inspectable, adjustable and replaceable;
- intended to retain laterally displaced coarse/mixed product on the belt.

The report should not prescribe fabrication-level detail such as exact compound, hardness, seal profile, contact pressure, clamp spacing, skirt height/length, structural attachments, wear allowance or installation dimensions.
