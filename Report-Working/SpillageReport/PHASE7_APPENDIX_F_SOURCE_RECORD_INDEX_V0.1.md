## F7. Source-control principle
Where source records, photographs/videos and later engineering interpretation differ in evidentiary level, the final report must preserve that distinction. A later synthesis may organise and explain the source evidence; it must not silently promote an unverified source-recorded statement or engineering hypothesis into a directly proven fact.
