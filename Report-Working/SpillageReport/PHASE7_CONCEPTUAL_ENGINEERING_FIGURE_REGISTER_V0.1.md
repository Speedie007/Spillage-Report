## Purpose
This register controls the conceptual engineering figures currently retained for the working report. These figures are **report-support engineering concepts**, not as-found inspection evidence and not fabrication drawings.

They may be used in the current report development to communicate the intended engineering principle. Detailed technical review, geometry refinement and engineer-requested adjustments are intentionally deferred until the report development/assembly is substantially complete.

## Governing qualification
All figures are to remain explicitly understood as:

> **CONCEPTUAL ONLY — NOT FOR FABRICATION — DIMENSIONS / GEOMETRY TO BE VERIFIED ON SITE**

Where the final report uses a figure, its caption or nearby text should preserve the same qualification.

## Evidence-status rule
These four figures are **not included in the controlled 197 inspection-media count**. They are derived report-support material and must not be cited as proof of an as-found physical condition.

Inspection photographs/videos remain the evidentiary source for physical findings. The conceptual figures may only explain an engineering interpretation, control philosophy or proposed principle already supported by the report body.

Before any figure is treated as final technical artwork or used for design/fabrication purposes:
1. review the concept with the responsible engineer;
2. record requested conceptual changes;
3. verify site geometry and required dimensions;
4. revise the figure where needed;
5. confirm the final report caption/qualification;
6. normalise final drawing IDs/filenames.

This technical refinement is deliberately deferred until the report development and principal content assembly are complete.
