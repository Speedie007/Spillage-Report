## CTD-005 / DRAW-005 — CV09 Preferred Primary / Secondary Cleaner Placement and Orientation

- **Controlled filename:** `Evidence/TechnicalDrawings/DRAW-005-CV09_Cleaner_and_Process_Containment_Concept.png`
- **Current binary status:** user-supplied figure accepted for report development; repository binary is to be synced under the controlled filename above.
- **Purpose:** show the **typical positional relationship and orientation** of primary and secondary belt-cleaner stages relative to the head/drive pulley and head chute.
- **What this figure is intended to communicate:**
  - cleaner stages should act in the head/discharge region;
  - the cleaner contact locations should be associated with belt that remains supported by the head pulley;
  - a primary and secondary cleaner can be arranged sequentially before the belt enters the return run;
  - scraped material can remain within the chute/discharge route.
- **What this figure does NOT define:** exact CV09 dimensions, OEM cleaner make/model, final angular positions, spring arrangement, support geometry, chute-wall slots, travel stops, structural details or fabrication geometry.
- **Report role:** principal external/reference figure for the **“where / orientation”** part of the CV09 cleaner philosophy.
- **Status:** **ACCEPTED AS CURRENT CV09 PLACEMENT / ORIENTATION REFERENCE.**

## CTD-006 / DRAW-006 — CV09 Cleaners Inside Head-Chute Containment Principle

- **Controlled filename:** `Evidence/TechnicalDrawings/DRAW-006-CV09_Cleaner_and_Process_Containment_Concept_Scrapper_contextual_scrapper_must_be_inside_the_dis-charge_chute.png`
- **Current binary status:** user-supplied/project-developed conceptual figure accepted for report development; repository binary is to be synced under the controlled filename above.
- **Purpose:** communicate the **containment relationship**: both cleaner stages are to be installed within the head/discharge chute so material removed from the belt falls naturally inside the existing combined-product discharge flow rather than into the surrounding plant.
- **What this figure is intended to communicate:**
  - the head pulley is enclosed by the head chute;
  - both cleaner stages belong inside that chute/discharge zone;
  - removed fines remain in the existing discharge path;
  - the concept does not require a separate scraper-fines recovery route;
  - typical primary and secondary cleaner forms are shown only as examples.
- **What this figure does NOT define:** exact CV09 mechanical configuration, OEM hardware, cross-arm/slot/tensioner details, component dimensions, final clearances or fabrication design.
- **Report role:** principal project conceptual figure for the **“inside which containment / why?”** part of the CV09 cleaner philosophy.
- **Status:** **ACCEPTED AS CURRENT CV09 CONTAINMENT-PRINCIPLE FIGURE.**

# CV09 Two-Figure Interpretation Rule — BINDING

The V4.2 locked CV09 technical concept is intentionally communicated using two complementary figures rather than attempting to place every mechanical detail in one drawing:

1. **DRAW-005 answers: “Where should the cleaner stages act and what is their typical orientation relative to the head pulley?”**
2. **DRAW-006 answers: “Where should those cleaner stages sit relative to the head chute so removed fines remain within the discharge flow?”**

The detailed V4.2 functional requirements — full transverse support, both-side chute penetrations/guided movement, external self-tensioning, positive travel/wear limitation, stable cross-belt contact, two-stage default / one-stage constrained minimum and OEM design boundary — remain controlled by the written context and are **not required to be fully depicted in either figure**.

No reader should infer that the hardware drawn in DRAW-005 or DRAW-006 is the prescribed OEM product or final CV09 fabrication design.
