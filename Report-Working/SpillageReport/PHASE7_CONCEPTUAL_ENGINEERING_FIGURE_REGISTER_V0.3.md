- **Purpose:** show the W10 conveyor in its tunnel context together with the accepted functional side-skirt / spill-seal concept.
- **What this figure is intended to communicate:**
  - W10 is located in a restricted tunnel/access environment;
  - the conveyor remains a troughed belt carrying coarse/mixed manganese ore;
  - soft side skirting / spill sealing is fitted on the **inside of the conveyor carrying width**, along the belt edges;
  - the skirting forms a near-vertical containment surface on each side of the carried product;
  - the lower skirt/seal region follows the troughed belt edge closely so laterally displaced product is encouraged to remain on the belt rather than escape over the side;
  - the tunnel/person context remains relevant as consequence/context, but **walkway protection is not the defining design objective of the skirting**.

# W10 Two-Figure Interpretation Rule — BINDING

The accepted W10 side-containment philosophy is intentionally communicated using two complementary drawings:

1. **DRAW-0010 answers: “Where is the skirting used in the W10 conveyor/tunnel context?”**
2. **DRAW-0011 answers: “What is the physical side-skirt / spill-seal mechanism in conveyor cross-section?”**

The governing W10 functional objective is:

> **Keep the transported product on the belt by providing side skirting / spill sealing within the conveyor carrying width, while separately investigating and correcting loading, tracking or other primary contributors where present.**

The restricted tunnel/access route remains relevant to the consequence and significance of W10 spillage, but it is **not** the defining purpose of the skirting system.

The filenames `DRAW-0010-W10_Side_Containment_Barrier_Principle.png` and `DRAW-0011-W10_Side_Containment_Barrier_Principle.png` are retained as supplied for file continuity. In report narrative/captions, use **side skirting / spill seal / product containment**, not external barrier terminology.

# W10 Functional / Detailed-Design Boundary

The audit/report may define:

- the need to retain laterally displaced coarse/mixed product on the belt;
- side skirting/spill sealing on both conveyor sides where required;
- installation on the inside of the carrying width;
- belt-friendly flexible containment/sealing behaviour;
- compatibility with normal troughed-belt movement and tracking;
- avoidance of a rigid external barrier interpretation;
- maintainability, inspection, adjustment and replacement accessibility;
- representative operating verification after installation.

The OEM/detailed designer should determine, as applicable:

- exact skirt/seal technology and profile;
- rubber/polymer/other wear-material grade and hardness;
- exact longitudinal extent and height;
- clamp/support system;
- contact or controlled-clearance arrangement;
- adjustment and wear compensation;
- belt-edge clearance/tracking accommodation;
- structural brackets/attachments;
- guarding/access details;
- installation dimensions and tolerances.
