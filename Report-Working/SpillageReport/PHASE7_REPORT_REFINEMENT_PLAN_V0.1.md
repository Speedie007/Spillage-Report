# South32 Wessels Combined Product Spillage Audit — Phase 7 Refinement Plan V0.1

## Status
**PHASE 7 — NARRATIVE / RECOMMENDATION REFINEMENT: CURRENT**

## Governing production baseline
Phase 7 starts from the inspector-approved V0.2 assembly draft. The accepted report journey is not to be redesigned unless a genuine technical contradiction or missing mechanism is discovered.

The governing rhythm remains:

> **Explain → Prove → Interpret → Act / Transition**

The purpose of Phase 7 is to make the assembled argument more technically actionable and complete before global styling begins.

---

## 1. Phase 7 Objectives

1. Preserve the seamless reader journey approved in V0.2.
2. Remove unnecessary repetition without flattening the narrative voice.
3. Strengthen transitions where a local case becomes a wider system conclusion.
4. Mature recommendations from broad direction into controlled engineering actions.
5. Define success criteria for pilots, corrective actions and verification tasks.
6. Separate immediate corrective work, inspection/measurement, design development, structural assessment and operational controls.
7. Populate the principal technical registers required to support implementation.
8. Develop conceptual figures only where they materially improve understanding; do not present concepts as final designs.
9. Complete a technical consistency pass before document-wide styling.

---

## 2. Phase 7 Production Passes

### Pass 7A — Narrative Continuity and Duplication Review
Review V0.2 from Executive Summary through Conclusion for:
- repeated statements that no longer add value;
- missing transition sentences;
- sections that arrive before their logic has been established;
- sections that over-explain a point already proven by the evidence module;
- terminology consistency;
- preservation of the approved multi-reader tone.

**Rule:** refine, do not rewrite the accepted report identity.

### Pass 7B — Recommendation Maturity
For each major case/action, define:
- engineering objective;
- action type;
- required inspection/measurement;
- design-development requirement where applicable;
- implementation dependency;
- success / acceptance criterion;
- evidence/case traceability;
- items that remain client/OEM/competent-person decisions.

Priority cases:
1. CV09 cleaner/containment pilot;
2. U4 tracking/alignment;
3. Sample Conveyor 3 feed/tracking/containment;
4. Sample Conveyor 2 feed/loading verification;
5. W10 side-containment feasibility;
6. Screens water escape + structural assessment;
7. L1 structural/tracking actions;
8. trip/restart and housekeeping governance.

### Pass 7C — Verification and Success-Criteria Register
Build a controlled verification register separating:
- measured geometry required;
- functional checks during representative operation;
- structural assessment requirements;
- design inputs;
- pilot monitoring requirements;
- close-out evidence required before an action can be considered complete.

No measurement is to be invented. Unknown values remain explicit verification items.

### Pass 7D — Conceptual Engineering Figures
Develop only the concepts needed to explain the control philosophy.

Priority concept candidates:
- CV09 cleaner + effective chute/process containment envelope;
- W10 local side-containment barrier/rail principle;
- Sample Conveyor feed / usable belt width / tracking / secondary containment hierarchy;
- simple process-containment / cause-location vs deposit-location explanatory figure if the text benefits from it.

Concept labels must state **conceptual / not for fabrication / dimensions to be verified** where appropriate.

### Pass 7E — Technical Backbone Population
Populate or prepare:
- Appendix A — 23-group asset audit matrix;
- Appendix B — controlled photographic evidence register;
- Appendix C — video evidence register;
- Appendix D — measurements / verification register;
- Appendix E — conceptual engineering drawings;
- Appendix F — source-record index.

Body readability remains protected: detailed registers stay in appendices rather than being pulled into the narrative.

### Pass 7F — Technical Consistency / Evidence-to-Action Review
Final content audit before styling:
- every material action traces to evidence or an explicitly qualified engineering need;
- no inference has been promoted to proven fact;
- no unsupported quantification has entered the report;
- systemic conclusions do not over-generalise local cases;
- local exceptions remain explicit;
- recommendation terminology is consistent;
- conclusion accurately reflects the body and priority programme.

## 3. Priority Success Criteria to Develop

### CV09 pilot
Success should be assessed against function, not appearance:
- reduced downstream carryback relative to the pre-trial condition;
- removed wet fines routed into the intended process stream rather than the plant area;
- stable cleaner-to-belt contact through the agreed wear range;
- no unacceptable belt damage or abnormal wear;
- maintainable inspection/adjustment access;
- no new trapping/guarding/safety conflict;
- monitoring period and inspection record sufficient to judge performance.

### U4 tracking/alignment
- relevant pulley/alignment geometry measured and corrected as required;
- belt tracking verified during representative operation and across full tripper travel;
- adequate side clearance/carrying margin demonstrated;
- one-sided product-loss condition materially reduced/removed;
- custom cleaner configuration assessed separately.

### Sample Conveyors 2 and 3
- usable belt width documented;
- representative feed quantity / product-size condition verified;
- Sample 3 lateral offset measured and root mechanical contributors checked;
- tracking/feed corrected before secondary containment is finalised;
- any side containment maintains required belt clearance and maintainability.

### W10
- normal belt tracking envelope established;
- trough/belt/support/clearance geometry verified;
- initiating contributors investigated;
- secondary barrier concept shown not to become a normal belt-contact surface;
- access, trapping, wear and maintenance risks assessed.

### Screens / L1 structural work
- competent structural assessment completed;
- condition, remaining capacity and required repair/replacement scope defined;
- water escape mechanism identified at Screens before detailed modification;
- structural repair recommendations separated from assumptions about the original cause.
