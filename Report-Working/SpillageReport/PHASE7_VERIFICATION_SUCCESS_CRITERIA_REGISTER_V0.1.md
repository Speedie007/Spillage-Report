# South32 Wessels Combined Product Spillage Audit — Phase 7 Verification / Success-Criteria Register V0.1

## Status
**PASS 7C — CURRENT CONTROLLED VERIFICATION REGISTER**

## Purpose
This register consolidates the open verification, measurement, design-input, representative-operation, structural-assessment, pilot-monitoring and close-out requirements arising from the refined report body and the reconciled Part XI action programme.

It is intended to provide one controlled answer to four questions for every major action:

1. **What still has to be verified?**
2. **Why is that verification required?**
3. **What decision or action is blocked until it is available?**
4. **What evidence is required before the item can be considered closed?**

No unknown dimension, tolerance, feed rate, structural capacity, OEM limit, monitoring period or acceptance threshold is invented in this register. Where a numerical criterion is required, the value remains a South32 / responsible-engineer / OEM / competent-person decision based on verified site conditions and applicable requirements.

This register is the principal Phase 7C source for **Appendix D — Measurements / Verification Register** during Pass 7E.

---

# 1. Verification Classification

The following verification classes are used throughout the register.

| Code | Verification class | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | Measured geometry / dimensional verification | Fixed-reference survey, belt/pulley/support geometry, clearances, usable belt width, cut/notch geometry, section-loss measurements or other dimensional inputs. |
| **O** | Representative operating verification | Live/operating observation under conditions representative of the mechanism being assessed, including full operating range where applicable. |
| **D** | Design-development input | Verified information required before a concept can be converted into a detailed engineered modification. |
| **S** | Structural assessment | Competent structural inspection, measurement, capacity assessment, repair/replacement definition and formal acceptance. |
| **P** | Pilot / performance monitoring | Before/after or trial monitoring sufficient to judge whether the intended functional result has been achieved. |
| **C** | Procedure / governance verification | Confirmation of actual procedure, responsibility, frequency, recovery route, recordkeeping or operational control. |
| **A** | Applicability review | Verification that a proven functional solution is suitable for another location before wider rollout. |

---

# 2. Gate Definitions

Verification items are tied to the point at which the information becomes necessary.

### Gate G1 — Before detailed design / final solution selection
Required information must be available before a concept is treated as a detailed design or before final hardware/geometry is selected.

### Gate G2 — Before implementation / repair acceptance
Required information must be available before fabrication, installation, adjustment, structural repair or operational implementation can be accepted as properly defined.

### Gate G3 — Before final functional close-out
The completed action must be demonstrated under the appropriate operating/assessment condition. Task completion alone is not sufficient.

### Gate G4 — Before wider rollout / standardisation
A local pilot or correction must first prove the intended function, and the next location must then pass a separate applicability review.

## VER-001 — CV09 As-Found Geometry and Pilot Design Inputs

**Action link:** Part XI §24.1 — CV09 cleaner / fines-containment pilot  
**Verification class:** M / D / O  
**Primary gate:** G1

### Verification required
Record the CV09 as-found condition sufficiently to develop the pilot from verified site geometry, including:
- existing cleaner arrangement, position and blade/contact condition;
- cleaner-to-belt relationship and available adjustment/travel arrangement;
- local chute/enclosure geometry and physical installation envelope;
- intended route for cleaner-removed wet fines to return to the process stream;
- maintenance, inspection, guarding and replacement access;
- representative pre-trial condition of downstream carryback and local wet-fines fallout.

### Why it is required
The evidence proves active wet-material fallout and an open/non-contained local cleaner arrangement, but it does not provide the dimensional basis for final chute modification, cleaner selection, cleaner travel/wear design or primary/secondary stage selection.

### Decision blocked until verified
- final cleaner type/configuration;
- final chute/enclosure geometry;
- final cleaner position/travel arrangement;
- fabrication geometry;
- trial baseline definition.

### Required close-out evidence for this verification stage
- dated as-found measurement/survey record;
- annotated photographs/sketches or controlled site geometry record;
- recorded intended fines-return path;
- documented access/guarding constraints;
- pre-trial operating-condition record suitable for later comparison.

### Evidence traceability
`CV09-REF-002`, `CV09-REF-003`, video `CV09-REF-006`, supplementary video `CV09-REF-008`.

### Authority boundary
Final geometry, cleaner selection, wear/travel limits and detailed design remain South32 / responsible-engineer / OEM decisions as applicable.

## VER-002 — CV09 Pilot Functional Performance

**Action link:** Part XI §24.1  
**Verification class:** O / P  
**Primary gates:** G3 / G4

### Verification required
Operate the completed pilot for an agreed representative monitoring period and compare the post-modification condition with the as-found baseline.

### Functional success criteria
The operating record must demonstrate that:
- downstream carryback is materially reduced relative to the pre-trial condition;
- wet fines removed by the cleaner remain within the intended process stream rather than falling into the surrounding plant area;
- cleaner-to-belt contact remains stable through the agreed operating/wear condition;
- no unacceptable belt damage or abnormal cleaner wear is introduced;
- inspection, adjustment and replacement remain practical;
- no unacceptable guarding, trapping or access conflict is introduced;
- the monitoring record is sufficient for a defensible decision on wider applicability.

### Decision blocked until verified
Wider rollout of the cleaner-plus-containment philosophy to other locations.

### Required close-out evidence
- post-installation commissioning record;
- representative operating observations;
- before/after photographic or video evidence where practical;
- inspection/maintenance records from the trial period;
- recorded comparison against the pre-trial carryback/fallout baseline;
- formal pilot outcome: successful / requires modification / unsuitable.

### Authority boundary
The monitoring duration and accepted performance level remain South32 / responsible-engineer decisions.

- site-specific design/engineering record;
- installation/commissioning record;

### Governing rule

# 5. Register-Wide Close-Out Rules

1. **No invented measurements.** Unknown dimensions, tolerances, capacities and operating limits remain verification items until measured/defined by the responsible authority.
2. **Representative operation matters.** A static centred belt, newly installed cleaner, repaired chute or clean floor does not by itself prove the operating mechanism has been controlled.
3. **Structural items require competent assessment.** Photographs demonstrate condition; they do not establish remaining capacity.
4. **Design concepts remain concepts until geometry is verified.** No conceptual barrier, skirt, chute extension or cleaner arrangement is a fabrication drawing.
5. **Evidence strength governs causal wording.** Verification may strengthen or modify a causal conclusion, but current uncertainties are not retrospectively promoted to fact without supporting evidence.
6. **As-left records are part of closure.** Where geometry or configuration is corrected, retain the final reference condition for future trending.
7. **Monitoring closes the loop.** Where recurrence is the issue, post-intervention operating/housekeeping evidence should demonstrate that the recurring consequence is materially reduced.
8. **Rollout is not automatic.** A successful pilot proves a function at one location; each subsequent location still requires applicability verification.

# 6. Pass 7C Completion Test

Pass 7C can be considered complete when:
- every major Part XI action has a corresponding verification/close-out entry;
- measured geometry, representative-operation checks, structural assessments, design inputs and pilot monitoring are separated explicitly;
- all unknown numerical values remain un-invented verification items;
- every major action has an identifiable close-out evidence package;
- rollout decisions are gated by functional proof and local applicability.
