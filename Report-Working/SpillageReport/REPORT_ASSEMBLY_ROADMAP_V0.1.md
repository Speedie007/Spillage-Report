## 3. Governing Assembly Rules

1. **Narrative first.** Explain the engineering proposition before presenting detailed proof.
2. **Evidence as proof.** E/S evidence supports the proposition; it does not dictate a flat asset-by-asset report structure.
3. **Interpret after proof.** State what the evidence means and retain what it does not prove.
4. **Transition deliberately.** Convert the local case into the next system-level engineering point or action.
5. **Do not give every asset equal narrative weight.** Use major cases as proof; supporting assets remain in system montages, comparator strips, summary tables or appendices.
6. **E does not mean every E item must be shown.** E items form the principal proof pool; final body use is determined by story need and layout value.
7. **S is optional body support, not mandatory content.** Use only when it adds mechanism, operating context, extent, comparison or design understanding.
8. **A remains controlled technical evidence.** Keep it available for appendices, traceability and alternate layout use.
9. **Preserve evidence boundaries.** Direct observation, visual proof, source-recorded information, engineering inference and working concepts must remain distinguishable.
10. **No unsupported quantification.** Visible extent supports severity/persistence but not inferred tonnage, percentage or alignment magnitude unless measured.
