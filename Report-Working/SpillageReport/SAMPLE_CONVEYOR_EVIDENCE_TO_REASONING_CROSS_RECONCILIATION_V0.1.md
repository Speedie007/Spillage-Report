# 1. Governing relationship framework

The Sample Conveyor evidence is to explain four interacting engineering relationships without claiming an unverified single initiating cause:

1. **Feed/loading condition** — the quantity/distribution of product presented to the narrow Sample Conveyor system.
2. **Product size versus available belt geometry** — coarse/lump product can occupy a substantial proportion of the usable carrying width.
3. **Lateral belt position / tracking** — lateral offset can reduce the usable carrying margin available to retain product.
4. **Containment loss and consequence** — product is visibly outside the intended carrying path and accumulates around the installation.

Still photographs do not prove live feed rate, rated design-capacity exceedance, tracking persistence/frequency or a measured offset percentage. Those remain verification items.

# 6. Proposed report reasoning after cross-image review

## Sample Conveyor 2

> **Observed coarse-product scale and loading occupy a substantial proportion of the narrow carrying width. The complementary photographs also support a lateral belt-offset condition at the captured state. Product is visibly outside the intended carrying path and the entry/guide zone is heavily congested. These conditions are consistent with interacting feed, product-size, tracking and containment mechanisms, but the photographs do not establish one chronological initiating cause.**

Required sequence:

1. establish representative feed quantity/variation and product-size distribution;
2. quantify belt offset/tracking and determine its cause;
3. inspect and clear/assess entry-zone congestion and possible belt interference;
4. correct confirmed tracking/alignment/interference defects;
5. verify whether feed quantity, product size or loading distribution also requires control;
6. reassess usable carrying geometry under representative operation;
7. establish residual containment duty; and
8. install belt-compatible side-skirt/spill-seal containment where required.

## Sample Conveyor 3

> **The valid evidence supports the same feed/product-size/narrow-belt relationship and additionally shows a visually substantial lateral belt offset. REF-004 provides the combined relationship summary, while REF-006/007 provide the principal tracking evidence and REF-012 demonstrates the accumulated consequence.**

Required sequence remains diagnostic rather than prescriptive:

1. establish representative feed quantity/variation and product-size distribution;
2. quantify the lateral offset/tracking under representative loading;
3. determine and correct alignment/tracking/interference causes;
4. establish usable carrying geometry after those corrections;
5. verify whether feed quantity/product size/loading distribution requires additional control;
6. reassess residual containment duty; and
7. install/adapt belt-compatible side-skirt/spill-seal containment where required.
