1. establish representative feed quantity/variation;
2. establish product-size distribution;
3. quantify tracking/offset under representative loading;
4. identify and correct alignment/tracking/interference causes;
5. inspect/clear displaced-product congestion and assess belt interference;
6. determine whether feed quantity/product size/loading distribution also requires control;
7. reassess usable carrying geometry;
8. determine residual containment duty;
9. install/adapt belt-compatible side-skirt / spill-seal containment where required; and
10. verify performance under representative operation.
