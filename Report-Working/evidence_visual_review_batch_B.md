# Visual Evidence Review - Batch B

## Scope
This review covers the second visual-evidence batch for the South32 Wessels Mine combined-product spillage report:

- Sample Conveyor 2 - 8 photographs
- Sample Conveyor 3 - 13 photographs
- Loading Station L3 Conveyor - 8 photographs

The review compares the uploaded photographs against the current contextual narrative and separates what is directly visible from what remains a source-recorded inspection observation or engineering interpretation.

## Evidence Classification Key
- **Direct** - the photograph itself clearly shows the stated physical condition.
- **Supporting** - the photograph supports the finding or mechanism but does not independently prove the full causal statement.
- **Context only** - useful for equipment/location orientation or inspected-condition record.
- **Source-recorded observation** - retained where the inspector recorded the condition directly but the supplied photograph does not fully prove or quantify it.

# 1. Sample Conveyor 2

## 1.1 Overall visual conclusion
The Sample Conveyor 2 photographs strongly confirm two physical conditions already captured in the contextual notes:

1. extensive spilled product around and beneath the conveyor; and
2. coarse/lump product occupying a large proportion of the available carrying width on the relatively narrow sample belt.

The photographs therefore strengthen the working conclusion that product size and local loading relative to the narrow belt are important contributors to containment loss.

The photographs do **not** establish the live feed rate, the upstream control philosophy, whether the conveyor is overloaded relative to its design capacity, or whether an adjustable upstream feed-control device is available. Those remain engineering/operational verification items.

Importantly, no photograph in this batch provides clear independent evidence of significant Sample Conveyor 2 mistracking. The existing V3 position that Sample 2 mistracking remains **unconfirmed** is therefore retained.

# 2. Sample Conveyor 3

## 2.1 Overall visual conclusion
The Sample Conveyor 3 photographs strongly confirm extensive solid/lump-product spillage and the same basic narrow-belt/product-size containment problem seen at Sample Conveyor 2. They also provide visual support for a lateral belt-tracking concern.

Several photographs show coarse product occupying a large proportion of the narrow belt width and material deposited immediately outside the intended belt path. The wider-area images also show substantial accumulations on surrounding grating/structure.

The tracking evidence is strongest in LoadoutStationSample3-REF-013, where the belt is visibly asymmetric relative to the side/training-roller arrangement, with one belt edge running close to the training/guide hardware while the opposite side has visibly greater clearance. REF-003 through REF-006 also provide supporting close-range evidence of an asymmetric belt position relative to the surrounding rollers/enclosure.

Accordingly, Sample Conveyor 3 mistracking is **visually supported/verified as a lateral offset condition**, but the photographs do not provide a reliable dimensional basis for the earlier approximate statement that roughly half of the usable carrying width was lost. Any percentage or millimetre offset must remain unquantified until measured.

The photographs also do not prove the mechanical root cause of the mistracking. Head/tail alignment, belt tension and trapped spilled product remain hypotheses/inspection targets rather than established causes.

# 3. Loading Station L3 Conveyor

## 3.1 Overall visual conclusion
The L3 photographs confirm dry-fines/material accumulation around the conveyor and provide visual support for the recorded tail-end belt/pulley offset. The strongest offset evidence is LoadoutStationL3-REF-001.

However, the photographs do **not** show a clear current solid/lump-product spillage mechanism at L3 comparable with Sample Conveyor 3. This is consistent with Questionnaire V1, which recorded dry fines/carryback but did not record solid/mixed-product spillage at L3.

Therefore the existing qualified position remains correct: L3 has an observed/visually supported lateral tail-end offset that warrants alignment investigation, but the evidence does not currently justify stating that this offset is directly causing significant solid-product spillage.

# 4. Batch B Comparison Against Contextual Notes

## 4.1 Findings strengthened by visual review
- Sample Conveyor 2: extensive product spillage is directly verified.
- Sample Conveyor 2: coarse/lump product occupies a substantial proportion of the narrow belt width; the size/loading/containment concern is directly supported.
- Sample Conveyor 3: extensive solid-product spillage and surrounding accumulation are directly verified.
- Sample Conveyor 3: coarse product occupies a substantial proportion of the narrow belt width.
- Sample Conveyor 3: lateral belt offset/mistracking is visually supported, strongest in REF-013.
- L3: dry-fines/material accumulation is directly verified.
- L3: tail-end offset is visually supported by REF-001.

## 4.2 Findings retained as unconfirmed or qualitative
- Sample Conveyor 2 mistracking remains **unconfirmed**.
- Exact upstream feed-control mechanism for Sample Conveyors 2/3 remains unknown.
- Neither Sample Conveyor 2 nor Sample Conveyor 3 photographs establish design-capacity overload in a quantitative engineering sense; they demonstrate large/coarse product relative to the available narrow belt width and significant containment loss.
- Sample Conveyor 3 approximate "~50% usable belt width" remains a qualitative visual estimate and should not be treated as a measurement.
- L3 approximate ">50% belt width" offset remains a qualitative inspector estimate, not a surveyed dimension.
- L3 direct causal contribution to solid-product spillage remains unconfirmed.

## 4.3 No material contradiction identified
No Batch B photograph materially contradicts the current contextual narrative. The most important refinements are:

1. **Sample Conveyor 2 mistracking should remain unconfirmed.**
2. **Sample Conveyor 3 mistracking is visually supported, but any percentage magnitude should be removed or explicitly labelled unmeasured.**
3. **L3 offset is supported, but the photographs do not establish a solid-product spillage causal chain.**
