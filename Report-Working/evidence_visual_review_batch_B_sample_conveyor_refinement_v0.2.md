# 1. Sample Conveyor 2 — refined conclusion

The focused review confirms:

1. extensive coarse/lump-product spillage and accumulated consequence;
2. coarse product occupying a substantial proportion of the narrow belt width;
3. direct containment loss in the entry/sample-conveyor zone;
4. a **visually supported lateral belt-offset condition at the captured state**, demonstrated by complementary REF-004 / REF-005 views; and
5. severe local congestion around the entry covers/guides in REF-006.

The earlier statement that Sample 2 mistracking was wholly unconfirmed is superseded. What remains unverified is the offset magnitude, persistence/frequency, load dependence and initiating cause.

# 3. Refined cross-asset engineering conclusion

The Sample Conveyor evidence now supports the following governing relationship:

> **feed/loading condition + product-size distribution + usable belt geometry + lateral belt position interact to determine whether coarse/mixed product can be retained on the narrow Sample Conveyor.**

The physical end conditions are consistent with multiple interacting mechanisms. The photographs are sufficient to define what must be investigated but are not sufficient to assign one universal initiating cause.

## Required investigation/action sequence

1. establish representative feed quantity/variation and product-size distribution;
2. quantify belt tracking/offset under representative loading;
3. determine and correct alignment/tracking/interference causes;
4. inspect and clear/assess displaced-product congestion and possible belt interference;
5. establish usable carrying geometry after primary corrections;
6. determine whether feed quantity, product size or loading distribution requires control;
7. reassess residual containment duty; and
8. where required, apply a belt-compatible side-skirt/spill-seal arrangement adapted from the W10 functional principle and verified under representative operation.
