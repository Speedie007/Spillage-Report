# Visual Evidence Review - Batch D

## Scope
This review covers the fourth visual-evidence batch for the South32 Wessels Mine combined-product spillage report:

- W10 - 12 photographs
- CV01 - 4 photographs
- CV02 - 4 photographs
- CV03 - 3 photographs
- CV04 - 5 photographs
- CV14 - 2 photographs
- CV15 - 8 photographs

The review compares the uploaded photographs against the current contextual narrative. Classification is based on what the media independently demonstrates. Where a condition such as a seized roller or wet carryback cannot be established from a static photograph alone, the distinction between visual evidence and source-recorded inspection observation is retained.

## Evidence Classification Key
- **Direct** - the photograph itself clearly shows the stated physical condition.
- **Supporting** - the photograph supports the finding or extent but does not independently prove the complete mechanism.
- **Context only** - useful for location/orientation or inspected-condition record.
- **Source-recorded observation** - an inspection observation retained from the questionnaires/contextual notes where the photograph supports but does not independently establish the complete proposition.

# 1. W10 Conveyor - Primary Feed

## 1.1 Overall visual conclusion
The W10 photographs strongly confirm substantial dry lump-product and fines accumulation within the enclosed conveyor tunnel. Product is visible along the narrow access route, below the conveyor and around support/return-side components. The photographs also clearly demonstrate the restricted-access character of the installation.

The media supports the existing W10 classification of dry fines, lump/solid product and mixed-size material. Dust/fines coating is visible on the belt and surrounding structure, supporting a carryback/fines condition, although the still photographs do not independently identify every item as having originated specifically from carryback.

The photographs show locally substantial accumulations within the inspected tunnel sections. The existing asset-level description of localised-to-moderate accumulation should therefore be retained as the inspection classification rather than reclassified solely from individual close views.

# 2. CV01

## 2.1 Overall visual conclusion
The CV01 evidence strongly confirms wet-fines/slurry and fines accumulation below the conveyor. CV01-REF-003 and -004 show large wet/damp fines piles and standing water/slurry in the surrounding area.

CV01-REF-001 clearly shows severe visible surface damage/abrasion on the return support roller identified in the inspection record. A static photograph cannot establish whether the roller was mechanically seized at the time of inspection. The technically stronger wording is therefore: **roller visibly damaged; seized condition remains a source-recorded inspection observation unless separately verified dynamically.**

The photographs do not provide strong direct evidence of significant lump/solid-product loss, consistent with Questionnaire V1.

## 2.2 Item review

# 7. CV15

## 7.1 Overall visual conclusion
The CV15 photographs strongly confirm extensive fines/carryback accumulation around chute E-3181 and the surrounding conveyor support structure. Material is deposited on grating, cross-bracing and structural members, demonstrating that the condition is distributed through the structure rather than limited to a single floor-level pile.

CV15-REF-003 directly identifies chute **E-3181**, confirming the location reference used in the contextual notes.

The supplied still photographs primarily show dry or dried fines deposits. They do not independently demonstrate the wet-fines component of the inspection classification at the moment photographed. The wet-fines component should therefore remain a source-recorded inspection observation rather than being described as visually obvious from this Batch D still set.

The images around the belt/discharge area provide context for the cleaner location, but the still photographs alone do not independently demonstrate cleaner effectiveness under operation. The existing assessment that the CV15 cleaner was observed and judged ineffective remains a source-recorded inspection conclusion unless supported by separate dynamic evidence.

## 7.2 Item review

- **CV15-REF-002:** Supporting / Context - closer view of the same belt/discharge/cleaner region with substantial fines contamination.
- **CV15-REF-003:** Direct context - chute marking `E-3181`; high value for location traceability.
- **CV15-REF-004:** Direct - large fines/carryback deposits accumulated on grating and structural cross-bracing. Very strong severity image.
- **CV15-REF-005:** Direct / Supporting - substantial deposits distributed through the support structure beneath the conveyor.
- **CV15-REF-006:** Direct - extensive fines/carryback coating and build-up across multiple cross-braces/support members. Very strong evidence of widespread structural contamination.

# 8. Batch D Comparison Against Contextual Notes

## 8.1 Findings strengthened by visual review
- W10 dry lump/mixed-product and fines accumulation in a confined/restricted tunnel environment is directly verified.
- CV01 wet-fines/slurry accumulation is directly verified.
- CV01 return support roller is visibly damaged/abraded.
- CV02 dry fines plus lump/mixed-product spillage is directly verified.
- CV03 clean/no-significant-spillage inspected condition is visually supported.
- CV04 wet-fines/slurry and extensive/widespread accumulation are strongly visually verified.
- CV14 trace/localised fines/carryback is visually supported.
- CV15 extensive distributed fines/carryback accumulation around E-3181 is strongly visually verified.

## 8.2 Important evidentiary refinements
- **CV01:** a still photograph cannot prove that the visibly damaged roller was seized. Retain `seized` as a source-recorded inspection observation unless a dynamic/mechanical verification source is cited.
- **CV15:** the supplied photographs strongly show fines/carryback accumulation, but the wet-fines component is not visually obvious in this still set. Retain wet-fines wording as source-recorded inspection information.
- **CV15 cleaner:** the photographs show the discharge/cleaner region and contamination, but do not by themselves prove operational cleaner effectiveness.
- **W10 extent:** individual photographs show locally severe piles in the tunnel, but the existing asset-level localised-to-moderate classification should not be changed solely from selected close views.

## 8.3 No material contradiction identified
No Batch D visual evidence materially contradicts the current contextual narrative. The principal value of this batch is completion of the broader Surface Screening evidence record and improved separation between visually proven physical conditions and inspection-recorded operational/mechanical conditions.
