# CV09 — Current Immediate Workstream

CV09 is the first solution to be technically refined.

Current established intent:

1. determine the preferred scraper location relative to the CV09 head/discharge/chute geometry and explain **why** that position is preferred;
2. keep the cleaner/removal point inside an effective revised/extended CV09 discharge-chute containment arrangement;
3. direct removed fines into the CV09 chute so they follow the fines discharge/stockpile route rather than entering the plant area;
4. define the conceptual spring-loaded/self-tensioning principle required to maintain positive contact as the wear element reduces in thickness;
5. define a positive mechanical wear/travel limit so the cleaner cannot over-travel toward the belt;
6. retain practical inspection, adjustment and replacement access;
7. determine whether primary/secondary cleaning is functionally justified;
8. prove the concept at CV09 before wider applicability review.

### OEM / detailed-design boundary

The audit should be specific about **required function, preferred conceptual location, operating principle, access, fines route, travel limitation and pilot performance**.

The OEM/detailed designer should determine fabrication-level details such as exact cleaner/blade type and material, component material grades/sizes, spring rates/preloads, shaft/pivot/support details, fasteners, wear limits, final dimensions, guarding, structural/fabrication details and commissioning instructions.

The report must not invent those OEM specifications.
