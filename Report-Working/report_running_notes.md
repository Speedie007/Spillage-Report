# Final engineering baseline

The issued report preserves the following accepted interpretation:

- fines accumulation was observed throughout the inspected conveyor ecosystem, with severity and local mechanism varying by location;
- carryback is the strongest recurring mechanism but is not claimed as the sole proven cause of every deposit;
- deposit location is not automatically the initiating cause location;
- housekeeping addresses accumulated consequence while source control aims to reduce recurrence;
- CV09 is the pilot/reference location for a cleaner-and-containment **functional standard**, not a universal identical-hardware prescription;
- W10 side skirting/spill sealing is the preferred engineered lateral-containment addition for the demonstrated W10 condition, with final design based on verified operating geometry;
- Sample Conveyors 2 and 3 use the same side-containment principle after feed/product/loading/tracking/alignment contributors are quantified and corrected or adjusted where required;
- U4 and L3 remain location-specific tracking/alignment cases;
- Screens water and structural findings remain separate follow-up workstreams;
- L1 structural and belt-clearance findings remain subject to competent assessment/verification;
- representative operating performance is the governing close-out principle.

Open measurements, detailed design, structural assessment, OEM review and operational verification recorded in the report are **implementation/client close-out actions**, not unfinished report development.
